About

Hi, I'm Yashvi Shah, a second-year B.Tech student specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at JECRC University, Jaipur. I am passionate about technology, software development, and exploring how AI and data-driven approaches can solve real-world problems.

My interests include AI, Data Science, logical problem-solving, and building practical, user-friendly applications. Through academic projects and initiatives such as MindMesh at the Smart India Hackathon, I am learning to turn ideas into meaningful technological solutions.

I value teamwork, leadership, creativity, and continuous learning. I enjoy collaborating with others, exploring new technologies, and developing solutions that address real community needs. My goal is to strengthen my technical skills and contribute to impactful projects.

I am based in Jaipur and open to suitable internship opportunities where I can learn, contribute, and grow as a technology professional.
